# Chrome Web Store — Kinyarwanda (rw)

## Izina

Avorythm — Gusimbuza amajwi ako kanya n'amagambo kuri videwo bya AI

## Ibisobanuro bigufi

Ubuhinduzi bwa AI kuri tabu wahisemo: gusimbuza amajwi ako kanya, inyandiko mu ndimi ebyiri n'igikoresho gikina kijyanirana.

## Ibisobanuro birambuye

Reba amasomo, videwo na filimi cyangwa wumve ibiganiro by'amajwi mu rurimi rwawe. Avorythm ikoresha AI mu guhindura amajwi ava kuri tabu ya mushakisha wihitiyemo. Ushobora kumva amajwi yahinduwe ako kanya, kubona amagambo yo mu rurimi rw'umwimerere n'ayahinduwe, cyangwa kugumana amajwi y'umwimerere ugasoma ubuhinduzi gusa.

Hitamo gukinira ibiriho ku ipaji y'umwimerere nta gutinda gukabije, cyangwa ukoreshe igikoresho gifata ibiriho n'igikinisha bijyanirana. Iyo uburyo bujyanyanisha ibiriho bukoreshejwe, gufata ibiriho bigenda imbere, mu gihe igikoresho cyigenga gikina gishobora guhagarara by'agateganyo, kwimukira ahandi mu byafashwe no kwerekana ku mugaragaro wose. Ushobora no kurangiza gufata ibiriho ubwawe.

Genzura ibintu bine ukwabyo: amajwi y'umwimerere, amajwi yahinduwe, amagambo y'umwimerere agaragara kuri videwo n'amagambo yahinduwe. Vanga ingano z'amajwi yombi, wimure kandi uhindure ingano y'ahagaragara amagambo, unohereze hanze videwo ya WebM wahinduye uko ushaka hamwe n'amagambo yo mu bwoko bwa SRT ari ukwawo. Uburyo busanzwe bwo gufata ibiriho, ushobora guhitamo gukoresha, bunabika amajwi yombi ukwawo muri WAV n'amagambo yombi ukwawo muri SRT.

Iyi nyongera ikora yigenga, idakeneye porogaramu yo kuri mudasobwa, Python, FFmpeg, localhost cyangwa igikoresho cy'amajwi kigana ikindi. Imigaragarire iriho iboneka mu Cyongereza, Igiperesi n'Igishinwa cyorohejwe gusa; ururimi rw'ubuhinduzi rwo ruhitwamo ukwaryo mu mahitamo 79 y'indimi.

Kuyitegura: shyiramo muri Settings urufunguzo rwawe rwa Gemini API ruvuye muri Google AI Studio, wemere ku bushake bwawe ko amajwi ya tabu wahisemo yoherezwa kuri Google Gemini, uhitemo ururimi maze ukande Start. Uburyo bw'inyongera bwo kunoza ubunyangamugayo bukoresha Groq Whisper mu kwandika ibivugwa, Gemini mu guhindura inyandiko na Gemini 3.1 Flash Live mu gutanga amajwi. Busaba urufunguzo rwa Groq, uruhushya rwo kugera ku rubuga rushobora gusabwa, n'ubwumvikane butandukanye bwo kohereza amajwi.

Ibanga: gufata ibiriho bitangira gusa umaze gutanga uburenganzira no gukanda Start. Amajwi ya tabu wahisemo n'inyandiko z'ibyavuzwe byoherezwa mu buryo butaziguye ku batanga serivisi za AI zikenewe kugira ngo ibyo wasabye bikorwe; ntibyoherezwa ku ukora Avorythm. Nta matangazo, isesengura ry'imikoreshereze cyangwa seriveri y'ubuhuza ikoreshwa n'uwakoze iyi nyongera.

Ubusanzwe, imfunguzo za API zigumaho gusa mu gihe uri gukoresha porogaramu. Ushobora guhitamo kwibuka urufunguzo rwa buri mutanga serivisi kuri iki gikoresho ukwacyo; ubwo buryo ntibuba bufunguye mu ntangiriro. Kopi zibitswe ntizijyanishwa n'ibiri ku bindi bikoresho kandi iyi nyongera ntizizifungisha umutekano. Guhagarika kwibuka urufunguzo bisiba kopi iri kuri iki gikoresho; gusiba urufunguzo bisiba kopi iri ku gikoresho n'ikoreshwa muri icyo gihe.

Uburyo busanzwe bwo gufata ibiriho bugatanga ibintu bine ntibuba bufunguye mu ntangiriro. Uburyo bujyanyanisha ibiriho bufatira amakuru kuri iki gikoresho kugira ngo akinwe cyangwa yoherezwe hanze, kandi bubika gusa ibyafashwe biheruka mu bubiko bwihariye bwa Chrome. Dosiye zavanyweho zibikwa muri Downloads/Avorythm.

Avorythm ni ubuntu kandi kode yayo irafunguye. Ingano y'ikoreshwa rya serivisi za AI zo hanze ritishyurwa n'uko imiterere yazo iboneka bishobora guhinduka. Gukora ako kanya bisaba igihe cyo guhererekanya amakuru ku muyoboro; ntibisezeranya ko nta gutinda kubaho cyangwa ko ubuhinduzi buzahora butunganye. Suzuma neza ibikubiyemo by'ingenzi. Ibirimo birinzwe na DRM n'amapaji y'imbere muri mushakisha bishobora kubuza gufata ibiriho.
