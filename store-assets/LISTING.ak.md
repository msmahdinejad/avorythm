## Din

Avorythm — AI kasa-dubbing ne nsɛm asekyerɛ

## Nkyerɛkyerɛmu tiawa

AI kyerɛ tab a woapaw no ase: live dubbing, nsɛm wɔ kasa abien mu, ne player a wɔahyɛ no nsɛ.

## Nkyerɛkyerɛmu a ɛkɔ akyiri

Hwɛ adesua, video ne sini, anaa tie podcast wɔ wo kasa mu. Avorythm de AI kyerɛ ɔdio a ɛfiri browser tab a woapaw no pɛpɛɛpɛ no ase. Tie kasa a wɔre-dub no wɔ bere koro no ara mu, hwɛ mfitiase ne nkyerɛase nsɛm ase, anaa ma ɔdio mfitiase no nkɔ so na kenkan nkyerɛase no nkutoo.

Paw playback a ɛma akyɛde sua wɔ kratafa mfitiase no so, anaa recorder ne player a wɔahyehyɛ wɔn bere yɛ pɛ. Synchronized capture di anim bere a player a ɛyɛ ne ho no tumi gyina, hwehwɛ baabi foforo na ɛda screen no nyinaa so. Wubetumi de nsa awie recording no.

Hwɛ akwan anan no mu biara wɔ ne ho: ɔdio mfitiase, ɔdio a wɔadub no, mfitiase nsɛm asekyerɛ ne nsɛm asekyerɛ a wɔakyerɛ ase. Fa ɔdio abien no gyinabea fra, tu na sesa caption overlay no kɛse, na yi WebM video a woahyɛ no sɛnea wopɛ ne SRT nsɛm asekyerɛ a ɛyɛ soronko. Sɛ wopɛ a, recording a ɛyɛ daa no nso sie ɔdio track abien no nyinaa sɛ WAV ne nsɛm track abien no nyinaa sɛ SRT.

Extension no yɛ adwuma ne ho, a desktop app, Python, FFmpeg, localhost anaa virtual audio device ho nhia. Interface no yɛ English, Persian ne Simplified Chinese nko ara; wopaw translation target no ne no ho wɔ kasa ahorow 79 mu.

Siesie: fa Google AI Studio mu Gemini API key a ɛyɛ wo dea hyɛ Settings mu, ma kwan pefee sɛ wɔde ɔdio a ɛfiri tab a woapaw no bɛkɔ Google Gemini, paw kasa bi na mia Start. Sɛ wopɛ mode a ɛyɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ a, ɛde Groq Whisper yɛ transcription, Gemini yɛ nsɛm nkyerɛase, na Gemini 3.1 Flash Live yɛ kasa. Ɛhia Groq key, host permission a wubetumi apaw, ne audio ho kwan a ɛyɛ soronko.

Kokoamsɛm: capture no fi ase bere a woama kwan na woamia Start nko ara. Ɔdio ne transcripts a ɛfiri tab a woapaw no kɔ AI providers a wɔhia ma processing a woabisa no nkyɛn tẽẽ, na ɛnkɔ Avorythm hwɛfo no nkyɛn da. Nnawɔtwe-mu-nna, analytics anaa relay server a developer no hwɛ so biara nni hɔ.

Sɛ wopaw no sɛnea ɛte fi ase a, API keys no yɛ session no dea nkutoo. Sɛ wopɛ sɛ wɔka provider key biara sie wɔ saa device yi so a, eyi yɛ nea wubetumi apaw no wɔ ne ho, na ɛyɛ adwuma-dum fi ase. Copies a wɔakora no ntumi nkɔ devices afoforo so na extension no nso mmfa encryption nsi wɔn so. Sɛ wudum remembering a, ɛpepa device copy no; sɛ wopopa key bi a, ɛpepa device copy ne session copy no nyinaa.

Recording a ɛma output anan no nyinaa yɛ adwuma no, wɔdum no fi ase. Synchronized mode no record wɔ device no so ma playback ne export, na ɛkora capture a etwa to no nkutoo wɔ Chrome private storage mu. Files a wo download no kɔ Downloads/Avorythm.

Avorythm yɛ ade a wontua ho ka na source code no abue. AI external services no free quotas ne model availability betumi asesa. Live processing hia network bere na ɛnhyɛ bɔ sɛ latency rentɔ koraa anaa nkyerɛase no bɛyɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ; hwɛ nsɛm a ɛho hia no mu bio. DRM-protected media ne browser nkratafa a ɛyɛ internal no betumi asiw capture kwan.
