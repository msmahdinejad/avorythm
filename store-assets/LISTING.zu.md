## Igama

Avorythm — Ukudabha Bukhoma Nemibhalo Engezansi nge-AI

## Incazelo emfushane

Ukuhumusha nge-AI kuthebhu oyikhethile: ukudabha bukhoma, imibhalo engezansi ngezilimi ezimbili nesidlali esivumelanisiwe.

## Incazelo eningiliziwe

Buka izifundo, amavidiyo namafilimu noma ulalele ama-podcast ngolimi lwakho. I-Avorythm isebenzisa i-AI ukuhumusha umsindo wethebhu yesiphequluli oyikhetha ngokucacile. Lalela amazwi ahunyushiwe bukhoma, buka imibhalo engezansi yolimi lokuqala nolimi oluhunyushiwe, noma ugcine umsindo wokuqala ufunde ukuhumusha kuphela.

Khetha ukudlala ngokubambezeleka okuncane ekhasini lokuqala noma usebenzise isirekhoda nesidlali esivumelanisiwe. Lapho uqopha ngendlela evumelanisiwe, ukuqopha kuhamba phambili kuyilapho isidlali esizimele singamisa isikhashana, siye engxenyeni oyifunayo futhi sibonise isikrini esigcwele. Ungakuqeda mathupha ukuqopha.

Lawula izingxenye ezine ngokuzimela: umsindo wokuqala, umsindo odabhiwe, imibhalo engezansi yolimi lokuqala nemibhalo engezansi ehunyushiwe. Lungisa amazinga ayo yomibili imisindo, hambisa futhi ushintshe usayizi wembondela yemibhalo engezansi, bese uthekelisa ividiyo ye-WebM oyilungiselele wena nemibhalo engezansi ehlukene ye-SRT. Ukuqopha okuvamile ongakukhetha nakho kulondoloza yomibili imisindo njenge-WAV kanye nayo yomibili imibhalo engezansi njenge-SRT.

Isandiso sisebenza ngokuzimela, ngaphandle kohlelo lokusebenza lwedeskithophu, i-Python, i-FFmpeg, i-localhost noma idivayisi yomsindo ebonakalayo. Isixhumi esibonakalayo samanje sisekela isiNgisi, isiPheresiya nesiShayina Esenziwe Lula; ulimi okuhunyushelwa kulo lukhethwa ngokuzimela ohlwini lwezilimi ezingu-79.

Ukusetha: faka ukhiye wakho we-Gemini API ovela ku-Google AI Studio kokuthi Izilungiselelo, uvume ngokucacile ukuthumela umsindo wethebhu oyikhethile ku-Google Gemini, ukhethe ulimi bese ucindezela u-Start. Imodi enembile ongayikhetha isebenzisa i-Groq Whisper ukubhala okukhulunywayo, i-Gemini ukuhumusha umbhalo ne-Gemini 3.1 Flash Live ukukhiqiza inkulumo. Idinga ukhiye we-Groq, imvume yokufinyelela kumsingathi ongayikhetha kanye nemvume ehlukile yokuthumela umsindo.

Ubumfihlo: ukuqopha kuqala kuphela ngemva kokuba uvumile futhi ucindezele u-Start. Umsindo wethebhu oyikhethile nemibhalo ebhalwe ngokukhulunywayo kuthunyelwa ngokuqondile kubahlinzeki be-AI abadingekayo ekucubunguleni okucelile, hhayi kumlondolozi we-Avorythm. Azikho izikhangiso, ukuhlaziywa kokusetshenziswa noma iseva yomlamuli eqhutshwa umthuthukisi.

Okhiye be-API bagcinwa ngesikhathi seseshini kuphela ngokuzenzakalelayo. Ukugcina ukhiye womhlinzeki ngamunye kule divayisi kuyinketho ezimele futhi kuvaliwe ngokuzenzakalelayo. Amakhophi agciniwe awavumelaniswa futhi awabethelwa yisandiso. Ukuvala ukugcinwa kususa ikhophi ekudivayisi; ukususa ukhiye kususa amakhophi asekudivayisini naweseshini.

Ukuqopha okuvamile okukhiqiza amafayela amane kuvaliwe ngokuzenzakalelayo. Imodi evumelanisiwe iqopha endaweni yedivayisi ukuze kudlalwe futhi kuthekeliswe, futhi igcina ukuqopha kwakamuva kuphela endaweni yokugcina eyimfihlo ye-Chrome. Amafayela alandiwe aya ku-Downloads/Avorythm.

I-Avorythm imahhala futhi ikhodi yayo ivulekile. Izabelo zokusebenzisa mahhala nokutholakala kwamamodeli ezinsizakalo ze-AI zangaphandle kungashintsha. Ukucubungula bukhoma kudinga isikhathi senethiwekhi futhi akuqinisekisi ukungabikho kokubambezeleka noma ukuhumusha okuphelele; qinisekisa okuqukethwe okubalulekile. Imidiya evikelwe nge-DRM namakhasi angaphakathi esiphequluli kungase kuvimbele ukuqopha.
